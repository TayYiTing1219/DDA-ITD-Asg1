# BirdHaven
BirdHaven is a sanctuary for bird lovers, bringing together fascinating facts, stunning photos, and detailed guides on birds from around the world. Explore, learn, and celebrate the beauty of our feathered friends.

### Gameplay in AR:
#### Breif description
- Users can scan an image of a bird and the 3D version of the bird will appear
- Users will be able to interect with the 3D bird and learn more facts about the bird in a more interactive way!

#### Features
- Interactive UI with intuitive controls for seamless navigation
- Detailed information covering facts, habitat, diet, and body parts (beak, feathers, wings) of Male and Female Kingfisher
- Feeding Mechanic: Offer virtual food to the 3D bird, triggering heart animations upon successful feeding
- Responsive touch for activating and toggling bird information
- AR positioning that keeps 3D models anchored to the scanned image
- Auto-adjusting UI that faces the user for optimal readability

#### How to use:
1. Launch the BirdHaven AR app on your device
2. Prepare a physical or digital image of Male/Female Kingfisher
3. Point your device's camera at the image to scan it
4. Wait for the 3D bird model to load and appear in your environment
5. Interact with the bird:
   - Tap the 3D bird to activate the interactive UI
   - Use the UI buttons to explore different categories: Facts, Habitat, Diet, Body Parts
   - Tap the “Feed” button at the bottom center of the app to offer virtual food tailored to the bird’s natural diet.
   - Watch as hearts pop up around the bird when it accepts the food
6. Close panels individually or tap the bird again to hide the entire UI
